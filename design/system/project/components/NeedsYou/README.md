The strip at the foot of every screen: what waits on you, one item each, each a button that lands on it.

**Markup**: `<button class="k-need k-need-ask"><span class="k-mk you"></span><b>Approve search-index</b><span class="k-need-sub">every check agreed · 12m</span></button>`.

**Kinds**: an ask (`k-need-ask`: `you-tint`, a 1px `you` edge, the `you` mark) and a part that stopped (`raised`, the `fail` mark). Nothing else reaches the strip: health and calibration notices wait in the inbox and never interrupt.

**Arrival**: an ask arrives once. It fades in from 20% `you` over `you-tint` (`k-arrive`, `dur-fresh`), the strongest start at which its title and line stay at 4.5:1 in both themes (measured), and the tab title gains its count, "(2) kstrl". A repeat adds to its item and stays quiet.

**Anatomy**: at least 44px tall, 12px inline padding, radius 10 (`radius-md`); the mark spans both lines; title 14px/600 (`body`); line 12px (`label` at 400) `text-2`. Hover deepens the tint (10% `you`); focus is the 2px `focus` ring.

**Narrower**: the strip stays one line. It sets aside its hint first, then needs from the right, and counts them on a `k-need-more` item ("2 more", "in the inbox"), which lands on the inbox; they come back when there is room. See Chrome for the order and the check.

**Don't**: put a notice (health, calibration) here; approve from here (it lands on the item, where the evidence is).
