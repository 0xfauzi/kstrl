The three bands every screen has, composed: the header, the title row and Needs you. The card is a working screen at 1280, the width of the frames: the crumbs, the zoom and Needs you all move it between levels, and the width control narrows it to 1024 and 768.

**Header** (`k-header`, 56px, padding 24): the logo; where you are, as crumbs (`k-crumbs`, `k-crumb`, the first `k-crumb-root`), each a button that zooms back out to its level, the last marked `aria-current="page"`; one level in (a spec, the inbox, settings), the standing conditions (`k-conds`), which the Factory level shows as tiles in its body instead and deeper levels leave out; then `grow`, whether the view is live (`k-live`), today's spend (`k-spend`, a floor, so ≥, in `measure`, with its cap in `text-3`) and the ask field (`k-ask`), which opens the command window, as ⌘K does from anywhere.

**Title row** (`k-titlerow` inside the content, at 32): an `h1.k-title` holding the title in the voice that owns it (`k-title-human` for a spec, in your words; `k-title-name` for a part, by its name, with the chip of the agent working on it), then one line of measured facts (`k-title-meta`, a spend in `v-measure`). On the right (`k-titletools`): a hint (`k-hint`), the level's views (Tabs) and the zoom (Segmented), which moves with the crumbs.

**Needs you** (`k-needs`, 72px, padding 24): the label, one `k-need` per thing waiting on you, and one hint (`k-needs-hint`). An approval opens over the screen with its evidence, never approved from the strip; a stopped part lands on its Part level. Every screen has it, with "Nothing yet" when nothing waits, except the Inbox, which is itself the list of what needs you and shows its keys in the band instead.

**Edges**: the header and Needs you pad 24; the title row and everything under it sit at 32, a step in from the bands around them. Every level page uses the same two.

**Narrower** (`kFit`, in the card's script): the header and Needs you stay one line and give way in a stated order, never by clipping. A child with `data-fit-drop="N"` is set aside (`data-fit-hidden`) in ascending N until the band fits, and comes back when there is room. The header sets aside the standing conditions (1), the cap after today's spend (2), the live text (3), then the ask field's words (4), keeping its keycaps and its name (`aria-label`). Needs you sets aside its hint (1), then needs from the right (4, 5), counted on a `k-need-more` item that lands on the inbox. The title row wraps its facts under the title; its tools never move. The crumbs and the spend always stay. The interaction test checks every level at 1280, 1024 and 768: each band exactly the screen's width, no overflow, the title clear of its tools.

**Keyboard**: Tab reaches the crumbs, the ask field, the views, the zoom and each need. A crumb is updated in place when you zoom, so the crumb you pressed keeps focus as it becomes the current one. ⌘K focuses the ask field.

**Don't**: rebuild the crumbs on a level change (focus falls to the page); let a band clip (a clipped ask field is a missing control); give the grid that holds the bands an `auto` column (a band then grows to its content and is clipped by the screen, and a fit check measured against the band passes while nothing fits: use `minmax(0,1fr)`).
