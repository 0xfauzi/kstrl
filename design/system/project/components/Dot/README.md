A small accent that is not one of the eight marks, always beside the word that says what it means: a fast check's result (tests, then typecheck, after each iteration), how something reaches you, a notice that only informs.

**Markup**: `<span class="k-dot k-dot-fail"></span>` beside its words.

**Anatomy**: 6px, round. One size: at 6px the ring variant's 1.5px stroke still leaves a hole, so it reads as a ring and not a smudge.

**Kinds**
- Default, `text-3`: sent by kstrl (a hook, a GitHub comment), a check that agreed, a notice that only informs.
- `k-dot-ink`, `text`: it interrupts you.
- `k-dot-ring`, a 1.5px `text-3` ring: it waits quietly.
- `k-dot-none`, `selected`: it does not reach you. At 1.19:1 on `window` by day and 1.24:1 at night (measured) it is meant to read as absence; the word beside it ("no") says so.
- `k-dot-fail`, `fail`: a check failed, with the words that say which.

**Changed from the frames**: three pages drew dots at 5, 6 and 7px, the channel matrix in four colours including `text-2`, which is 1.44:1 from `text-3` in both themes (measured): at 7px the two could not be told apart.

**Don't**: use a dot without its word; use one for a state that has a mark (needs you, working, waiting, agreed, disagreed, merged, skipped, not measured).
