Proposal: the factory level, the top of the zoom, showing what the factory has built, what it is building, what comes next and why it has not started, and the conditions that apply to all of it.

**Why this shape.** `ks serve` runs one spec at a time (it holds the factory lock for the whole run), so the factory's real order is built, building, next, and the page is those three tiles left to right. The spec being built is the hero: its plan drawn the way the Step level's Grid draws it, with each part named and in its state, and four counts under it. A built spec is drawn as the shape of its plan, small, with a total under the three. A spec in the queue has no plan yet (the architect runs when it starts), so it is your first sentence, in a dashed tile because nothing is working on it; under the queue are the checks `ks serve` makes before it takes the next spec, in its own order, and the first one not met is the reason nothing has started. The bottom row is the factory's conditions as four tiles (trust, spend today, main, learning); at this level they are not repeated in the header.

**Trust** draws the 15 clean merges L2 needs before L3 is offered as 15 ticks, 9 filled. **Learning**'s "≥27 referenced" is `measure_fact_utilization`, a substring match that undercounts paraphrase, so it is written as a lower bound.

**Built on**: `.kstrl/runs/` for built specs (parts, merges, cost); the running spec's `manifest.json` and events; the queue items (spec, source, priority, merge disposition: "merges wait for you" is `stop_at_pr`); `ks serve`'s admission checks (pause marker, poison streak, daily budget, inbox cap, parked merges, open PR bound, the factory lock); `autonomy.json` and its thresholds (placeholders in the code; promotion is terminal only); the spend ledger; safe mode's signals; CI on main; knowledge facts and fact utilization; `ks evolve` patterns.
